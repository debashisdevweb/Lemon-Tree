High-speed WiFi, a full-sized working desk, tea and coffee maker, LED TV with DTH, electronic safe, minibar, air conditioning, and a telephone with global dialling and voicemail — in every room.

Citrus Cafe serves multi-cuisine all day from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, with a breakfast buffet until 11:00 AM. Amilo, the rooftop bar, has an open-air gallery over the garden — the front desk has current hours. Room service runs alongside both.

Salugara Monastery is 1.5 km away, Bengal Safari 4.3 km and Hong Kong Market 4.6 km. Further out: Kalimpong at 61 km, Darjeeling and the Dooars at 70 km, and Gangtok at 108 km.
